# Project 1: Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview
Comprehensive analysis of 12,594 retail transactions across 24 months (2023–2024), 
covering 6 product categories and 5 geographic regions.

## Key Results
- Total Revenue: ₹26.08 Crores | Total Profit: ₹6.12 Crores (23.5% margin)
- YoY Growth: Strong upward trajectory across all regions
- Top Category: Electronics | Best Margin: Beauty & Personal Care (48%)
- Best Region: West | Peak: Q4 Festival Season
